Examination: Screening Bilateral Digital Mammography with Tomosynthesis

Indication

59-year-old female presenting for routine annual screening mammography. No current breast complaints. No palpable masses. Family history negative for breast cancer.

Comparison

Prior bilateral screening mammogram dated February 22, 2023, is available for comparison. Previous examination was assessed as BI-RADS Category 1 (negative).

Technique

Full-field digital mammography with digital breast tomosynthesis (DBT) was performed. Standard CC (craniocaudal) and MLO (mediolateral oblique) views were obtained of both breasts. Total glandular dose is within ACR guidelines. Images are of diagnostic quality.

Breast Composition

The breasts are heterogeneously dense, which may lower the sensitivity of mammography (ACR Breast Density Category C).

Findings

RIGHT BREAST:

There is a new suspicious spiculated mass measuring approximately 1.4 cm in greatest dimension located in the upper outer quadrant of the right breast at the 10 o'clock position, approximately 4 cm from the nipple. The mass demonstrates irregular margins with prominent spiculation radiating into the surrounding parenchyma. The lesion is associated with a cluster of pleomorphic microcalcifications extending over an area of approximately 1.8 cm. This finding was not present on the prior examination dated February 2023.

No other suspicious masses, architectural distortion, or abnormal asymmetries are identified in the right breast.

LEFT BREAST:

Scattered fibroglandular tissue without suspicious masses, architectural distortion, or suspicious calcifications. The left breast is unchanged from the prior examination and demonstrates benign scattered parenchyma.

AXILLAE:

Normal-appearing lymph nodes are visualized bilaterally without pathologic features.

Impression

BI-RADS CATEGORY 4C – HIGH SUSPICION FOR MALIGNANCY

Right breast, upper outer quadrant, 10 o'clock, 4 cm from nipple: New 1.4 cm spiculated mass with associated pleomorphic microcalcifications. The morphology and appearance are highly suspicious for malignancy. Positive predictive value for this category ranges from 50% to 95%.

Left breast: Negative (BI-RADS 1).

Recommendation

Immediate further evaluation is recommended. Image-guided core needle biopsy of the right breast mass with sampling of the associated calcifications is indicated. Ultrasound correlation is recommended to assess for sonographic visibility of the lesion and to determine optimal biopsy approach (ultrasound-guided versus stereotactic).
